# GeoWake Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 22 August 2026**

## Summary

GeoWake does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything the app stores
stays on your device. There is no account, no server, and no analytics tracking your
movements.

## Location data

GeoWake needs your device's location to do the one thing it exists for: sound an alarm when
you arrive at a destination you chose.

**How it is used.** When you set an alarm, GeoWake asks Android to watch for your arrival at
a circular area around your destination. Android's own location services do this monitoring.
GeoWake is only woken up when you cross into that area.

**Background access.** GeoWake requests background location permission because the alarm has
to work when your phone is locked and in your pocket — which is the entire point of the app.
Without it, the alarm can only fire while you are actively looking at the screen.

**Where it goes.** Nowhere. Your location is never sent off your device, never written to a
server, never shared with third parties, and never used for advertising or profiling. GeoWake
has no backend to send it to.

**What is stored.** The destinations you save — a name, coordinates, an address label, and an
alert radius — are stored in your device's local app storage. They are deleted when you delete
them in the app, or when you uninstall GeoWake.

## Maps

Map tiles are provided by Google Maps. When the map is displayed, Google may receive data
about the map area being viewed, governed by
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). GeoWake does not send Google
your saved destinations or your alarm history.

## Crash reporting

GeoWake uses Sentry to report crashes and errors so they can be fixed. Crash reports contain
technical diagnostic information — device model, operating system version, and the sequence of
events leading to the failure. They do not contain your saved destinations or your location
coordinates.

## Permissions GeoWake requests

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Location (while in use) | Show your position on the map and let you pick a destination |
| Location (background) | Detect arrival and sound the alarm when the app is closed or the phone is locked |
| Notifications | Deliver the arrival alarm |
| Full-screen intent | Show the alarm over the lock screen so it can wake you |

You can revoke any of these at any time in Android Settings → Apps → GeoWake → Permissions.
Revoking location or notification permission will stop alarms from working.

## Children

GeoWake is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change with it. Material changes
will be noted in the app's release notes.
